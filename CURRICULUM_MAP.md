# Curriculum Map: Cambridge 9709 (Mechanics) & 9702 (Physics)

This index maps the latest Cambridge International AS & A Level syllabuses to visual simulation modules and common student misconceptions.

## 1. Mathematics 9709 — Paper 4: Mechanics (2026–2027)
- **Format:** 1h 15m | 50 marks | 6–8 structured questions

| Code | Syllabus Topic | Key Visuals & Components | Common Student Traps |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `9709-M1` | Forces and equilibrium | 2D $x/y$ & slope $\parallel/\perp$ vector triangles, $\mu R$ meter | Swapping $\sin\theta$/$\cos\theta$; assuming $F = \mu R$ before limiting equilibrium |
| `9709-M2` | Kinematics in a straight line | Live $s$-$t$ and $v$-$t$ graphs, area shading ($+$ vs $-$) | Confusing distance and displacement; using SUVAT for variable $a$ |
| `9709-M3` | Momentum | 1D collision track with signed velocity vectors | Sign errors on rebound; confusing momentum with KE |
| `9709-M4` | Newton's laws of motion | Split Free-Body Diagrams for pulleys, lifts, towing | Combining two bodies on different planes into one $F=ma$ |
| `9709-M5` | Energy, work and power | Energy bar chart ($\text{KE} + \text{GPE} + W_f$) & $P = Fv$ | Missing work done against friction; assuming constant $F$ at constant $P$ |

## 2. Physics 9702 (2025–2027)

### AS Level (Topics 1–11, 2025–2027 numbering)
- **1:** Physical quantities and units (incl. 1.3 errors & uncertainties, scalars & vectors)
- **2–5:** Kinematics (2D projectiles), Dynamics (momentum, Newton's 3rd law pairs, drag qualitative), Forces/density/pressure, Work/energy/power
- **6:** Deformation of solids
- **7–8:** Waves ($I \propto A^2$, Doppler, polarisation) & Superposition (standing waves, $d\sin\theta = n\lambda$)
- **9–10:** Electricity & D.C. circuits ($V = E - Ir$ terminal p.d. vs lost volts)
- **11:** Particle physics (nuclear decay, quarks)

### A Level (Topics 12–25)
- **12–13:** Circular motion ($T \pm mg$ in vertical circles) & Gravitational fields ($\phi = -GM/r$)
- **14–16:** Temperature, Ideal gases & Thermodynamics
- **17:** Oscillations / SHM (phasor circle, $x$-$v$-$a$ phase shifts, radian mode)
- **18–21:** Electric fields, Capacitance, Magnetic fields ($F = BQv\sin\theta$), Alternating currents (rectification)
- **22–25:** Quantum physics, Nuclear physics, Medical physics, Astronomy & cosmology
